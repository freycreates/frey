LONG (about 170 words)
FREY. is an Amsterdam-based DJ and multidisciplinary creative, raised on pop culture, queer nightlife and the internet.

FREY. started DJing in 2015 and played until 2017. After a hiatus, with a pandemic somewhere in the middle, FREY. found the way back behind the decks at a friend's Halloween party in 2022. That night was a reminder of how much was missed: building a set, reading a room and sharing pop moments with a crowd.

Since then, FREY. has become a regular at queer clubs, gay club nights and festivals in Amsterdam, Rotterdam, across the Netherlands, Brussels and Singapore. Sets start from pop and go wherever the room wants: Gaga, hyperpop, four-to-the-floor, remixes and own edits. Big singalongs and harder peaks, all in one night.

Next to the decks, FREY. works in social media, content and design.

HOW TO WRITE THE NAME
FREY. in capitals, with the full stop, every time.
At the end of a sentence one full stop is enough: "Booked: FREY." Not "FREY.."
Not: Frey, FREY, Frey.
