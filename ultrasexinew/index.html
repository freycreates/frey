FPQ

F*cking Pop Queers

An Amsterdam party that plays with nightlife and theme-party clichés.

It’s fun! It’s pop! And soo gay!

FPQ started at Studio80 and grew into its bigger sibling FPQ XL, which has been at Paradiso since 2011, usually twice a year.

About

U•S, Ultrasexi
Since 2005

A cooperative creative and production platform in Amsterdam, working with and for young talent.

We connect, encourage, inspire and support young creatives across creative fields. We use parties and nightlife to push towards a more creative, free-spirited society.

Founder: Dick Koopman

Milestones
  1. FPQ

    Sold out Paradiso every Pride edition.

  2. 2013

    FPQ has its own area at the Milkshake festival and a one-off stage at Valtifest. In September Xenomaniax starts at the Sugarfactory.

  3. 2011

    Ultrasexi at Paradiso makes way for FPQ XL.

  4. 2008

    The third birthday of the party is held as Ultrasexi at Paradiso, and becomes a Gay Pride Saturday tradition. Ultraviolet launches at Flexbar, with art, music, light and fashion.

  5. 2005

    On 5 August, during Gay Pride, it all starts as M.U.L.T.I.S.E.X.I. at Studio80.
