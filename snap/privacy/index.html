FREY. Lineup Snap

Privacy.

Last updated 17 October 2026

Lineup Snap is made by FREY., a sole proprietorship in the Netherlands (KVK 94022607). That's me, the DJ who builds it. You can reach me at hello@freycreates.nl.

The short version: your photos, logo and line-up never leave your device. There's no account, no ads and no tracking in the app.

The app.

Everything you add stays on your iPhone: your name, logo, photo, dates and venues. I don't receive any of it, and nothing is uploaded to a server of mine.

On your phone. Lineup Snap remembers your look, your saved venues and your past line-ups, so you don't have to type them again.

Your iCloud. Your look, name, logo, saved venues and past line-ups (with their dates) are also backed up to your own iCloud with Apple's key-value storage, so a new phone can restore them. Your background photo is not part of it. It sits in your Apple account. I can't see it. If you don't use iCloud, it simply stays on your phone.

Photos. The app can only add images to your photo library, and only when you tap Download. It can't read your photos. When you pick a background photo, you choose it yourself in the system picker. If you choose to take a photo, the camera asks for permission first.

Sharing. An image only leaves your phone when you share it yourself, for example to Instagram. From that moment the other app's own privacy policy applies.

Fonts. The fonts are inside the app. Only if you search for an extra font, the app asks Google Fonts for it. That sends the font name and your IP address to Google.

Lineup Snap Pro.

Pro is a yearly subscription that removes the corner badge from your images. The payment goes through Apple. I never see your name, card or any payment details. Apple tells the app whether Pro is on, and sends me anonymous sales reports. You can cancel any time in your Apple account settings.

The website.

The Lineup Snap page at freycreates.nl/snap works the same way: it runs in your browser and your photos, logo and line-up are never uploaded. Your look is saved in your own browser, nowhere else.

I count visits with GoatCounter, which doesn't use cookies and doesn't follow you around. It records things like which page was opened, from which country, and which browser. It can't identify you. The page also loads its fonts from Google Fonts, so Google sees your IP address when it does.

If you email me, I get your email address and what you wrote. I only use it to reply, and delete it when we're done.

Your rights.

You can ask me at any time to see, correct or delete your details. Email hello@freycreates.nl and I'll do it within a month. I hold very little: the app stores nothing with me, so in most cases there's nothing to delete. To remove what's on your phone, delete the app. To remove the iCloud backup, switch off iCloud for Lineup Snap in your iPhone settings. If you think I'm getting something wrong, tell me first and I'll sort it out. You can always go to a data protection authority too: the Autoriteit Persoonsgegevens in the Netherlands, or the one in your own EU country.

Changes.

If this changes, the date at the top changes too.
