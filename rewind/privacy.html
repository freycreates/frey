Privacy.

Last updated 7 October 2026

DJ Rewind is made by Freycreates, a sole proprietorship in the Netherlands. You can reach us at hello@freycreates.nl.

The beta sign-up

When you join the beta on this page, we collect:

  • your name,
  • the email address of your Apple ID,
  • which players you use and what you play,
  • your Instagram handle, if you fill it in.

Why: to invite you to the DJ Rewind beta through Apple's TestFlight, to email you about the beta, and to know which players to test. We only do this because you ticked the box to agree.

Who sees it: the form is sent to us by Formspree, which delivers it to our inbox. To send the TestFlight invite, your email address is shared with Apple. We never sell your details or use them for anything else.

How long: until the beta ends and DJ Rewind is in the App Store, and at most 12 months after that. Then we delete them.

The app

DJ Rewind reads the play history on your USB stick and keeps everything on your iPhone. There's no account, and nothing is uploaded to us. A card only leaves your phone when you share it yourself, for example to Instagram. DJ Rewind never changes anything on your USB stick.

Your rights

You can ask us at any time to see, correct or delete your details, or withdraw your consent. Email hello@freycreates.nl and we'll do it within a month. If you think we handle your details wrongly, you can complain to the Autoriteit Persoonsgegevens.
