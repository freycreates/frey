Questions.

Why an iPhone app and not a website?

No laptop, no upload. Just the phone that's already in your pocket after the gig. Your iPhone reads the stick directly, your stories are right there, and nothing gets uploaded.

Do I need a Mac or laptop?

No. Everything happens on your iPhone. Plug in the USB stick you played from, with an adapter if your iPhone needs one.

Does it change anything on my USB stick?

Never. DJ Rewind only reads your play history. Your stick, playlists and cue points stay exactly as they were.

Which sticks and players work?

USB sticks exported from rekordbox that you played from on club players. During the beta we're testing as many players as we can, so tell us which ones you use.

Where does my data go?

It stays on your phone. There's no account, and nothing is uploaded unless you share a card yourself.

What does it cost?

The basics are free, for good: your newest month, your set after every gig and your tracklist as text. Premium unlocks themes, cards with your DJ name, wedding and private-event cards and your full-year Rewind, for a monthly or yearly fee. During the beta, it's all free.
